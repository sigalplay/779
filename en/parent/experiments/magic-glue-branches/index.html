Magic Color Branches

Materials:
1Shallow plate or tray base
2White liquid glue
3Food coloring
4Cotton bud
5A little dish soap
Steps:
1Cover the base with a thick layer of white glue.
2Add two or three drops of coloring in the center without mixing.
3Dip a cotton bud in dish soap and touch the center of the color.
4Watch the color move outwards in branching lines.
5Leave it to dry for one or two days if you wish to keep the picture.

What's happening? Soap changes the surface tension at the contact point, making the color move quickly outwards in a branching pattern.
