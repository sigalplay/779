Magic Cracking Ice

Materials:
1Small bowl
2Water
3Blue food coloring
4Talcum powder, baby powder, or cornstarch
5A little liquid dish soap
6Cotton bud
Steps:
1Pour in enough water to cover the bottom.
2Add blue food coloring and mix.
3Dust a thin, dry layer of powder over the entire surface.
4Dip a cotton bud in dish soap.
5Touch it to the water and watch cracks appear as the powder moves aside.

What's happening? The powder floats on the surface tension of the water. Soap breaks that tension at the contact point, moving the water and powder rapidly outwards.
