Inflating Strawberry Balloon

Safety first: Adult supervision is required. Keep the balloon away from faces and stop if it becomes very large.

Materials:
1Clear balloon
2Small food bag
33–4 tablespoons vinegar
42 teaspoons baking soda
5Red food coloring
6Black marker
Steps:
1Fill the small bag with vinegar and coloring and tie it securely.
2Draw strawberry seeds on the balloon.
3Place the sealed bag inside the balloon.
4Add baking soda and tie the balloon securely.
5Gently crush only the inner bag and watch the balloon inflate.

What's happening? When vinegar and baking soda meet, they produce a gas that fills and inflates the balloon.
