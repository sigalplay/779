Colorful Foam Worm

Safety first: An adult cuts the bottle. Blow outwards only; never breathe in through the bottle.

Materials:
1Small plastic bottle
2Scissors
3Wet wipe
4Elastic band
5Food coloring
6Plate
7Water
8Dish soap
Steps:
1Adult: cut the bottle in half.
2Cover the cut end with the wipe and secure it with the elastic band.
3Add stripes of food coloring to the wipe.
4Mix water with a little dish soap and dip the covered end.
5Blow gently through the bottle opening and watch the foam worm grow.

What's happening? Air passes through tiny holes in the wipe and creates many connected soap bubbles.
