Board games

Board games

Classic games with pieces, steps, and ideas for making play easier or more challenging.

Funny Bunny

Race your rabbits to the carrot while the hill changes beneath them.

Age 5–8 15 min Easy

Pull the Carrot

Pull carrots from the hill and be ready for the rabbit to jump.

Age 4–8 10 min Easy

Jenga

Remove wooden blocks and place them on top without toppling the tower.

Age 6–9 20 min Medium

Domino Rally

Build a domino route and start a satisfying chain reaction.

Age 5–9 20 min Medium

Tap Tap

Use shapes, pins, and a small hammer to build a picture on cork.

Age 4–8 20 min Easy

Dancing Caterpillar

Use tongs to place marbles on a moving caterpillar.

Age 5–8 10 min Medium

Who Am I?

Ask questions to discover the picture you cannot see.

Age 4–9 15 min Easy

Guess Who?

Ask yes-or-no questions to identify your opponent's character.

Age 6–9 15 min Medium

Twister

Follow the spinner and place hands and feet on colored circles.

Age 5–9 15 min Medium

Submarines

Gently lift colorful submarines out of the sea with tongs, without touching the snakes on the surface.

Age 4–8 15 min Medium

Monkey Tree

Remove sticks carefully without making the monkeys fall.

Age 4–8 10 min Easy
