Strawberry-Banana Smoothie

Ingredients: (Full batch)
1One banana
2One cup of strawberries
3One cup of milk
Tools:
1Knife — adult use only
2Chopping board
3Blender — with adult supervision
4Cup
Steps:
1Carefully cut the banana and strawberries into pieces.
2Place the fruit and milk in the blender.
3Close the lid and blend until smooth.
4Pour into a cup and drink.
