Chocolate Lollipops

Ingredients: (Full batch)
1100 g chocolate
22 tablespoons decorative sprinkles
36 popsicle sticks
Tools:
1Food bag
2Bowl of hot water
3Baking paper
4Scissors
5Refrigerator
Steps:
1Place chocolate pieces in a bag and seal it.
2Put the bag into a bowl of hot water and wait for the chocolate to melt.
3While it melts, draw a simple shape on baking paper.
4Make a small hole in the bag, pipe chocolate over the shape, and add a popsicle stick.
5Add sprinkles.
6Place in the refrigerator to set.
