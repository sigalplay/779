Easy recipes for kids

Looking for recipes for kids that are clear and easy to follow? These illustrated recipes break each dish into simple steps, helping children take part in preparing food at home with adult supervision.

Choose an activity from the recipe collection to find ingredients, pictures, and step-by-step directions. These easy recipes for kids are designed to support independence, planning, sequencing, and fine motor practice while keeping the experience playful.

Are these recipes suitable for children?

Yes. The directions are visual and simple, but an adult should always supervise and handle heat, sharp tools, and any ingredients that require extra care.
