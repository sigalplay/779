Mug Cake

Ingredients: (Full batch)
14 tablespoons plain flour
22 tablespoons sugar
31 tablespoon cocoa powder
4¼ teaspoon baking powder
53 tablespoons milk or water
62 tablespoons oil
7¼ teaspoon vanilla extract — optional
Tools:
1Large microwave-safe mug
2Tablespoon
3Microwave
Steps:
1Place all the dry ingredients in the mug.
2Add the milk, oil, and vanilla.
3Mix well until smooth and free of lumps.
4Microwave on high for 60–90 seconds.
5Remove carefully — the mug is hot — and let it cool briefly before tasting.
