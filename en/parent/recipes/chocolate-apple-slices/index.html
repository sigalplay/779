Chocolate-Dipped Apple Slices

Ingredients: (Full batch)
13–4 large apples
212–16 wooden skewers
3200 g chocolate
4Optional sprinkles
Tools:
1Knife — adult use only
2Paper towel
3Plate
4Microwave-safe bowl
5Microwave — with adult supervision
Steps:
1Cut each apple into four or six thick wedges.
2Dry the apple wedges completely with paper towel so the coating sticks well.
3Gently push a wooden skewer into the bottom of each wedge.
4Melt the chocolate in the microwave in 30-second bursts, stirring each time.
5Dip the top half of each wedge in chocolate and add toppings before the coating sets.
