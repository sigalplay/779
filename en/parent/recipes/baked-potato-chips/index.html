Baked Potato Chips

Ingredients: (Full batch)
14–6 potatoes
22 tablespoons oil
3½ teaspoon coarse salt
Tools:
1Bowl of cold water
2Knife — adult use only
3Colander
4Two towels
5Baking tray with baking paper
6Oven — adult use only
Steps:
1Adult: slice the potatoes very thinly.
2Arrange in one layer on baking paper and lightly oil both sides.
3Adult: bake at 200°C for 15–18 minutes, then at 150°C for about five more minutes.
4Cool on a rack and sprinkle with a little coarse salt.
