💡Benefits of a weekly visual schedule

A weekly visual planner supports advance planning, prioritising tasks, managing time, and solving problems that may arise during the week.

  • Acts as an external memory aid, so more cognitive resources stay free.
  • Reduces anxiety and builds a sense of security – seeing the week ahead creates order, predictability, and certainty.
  • Encourages independence – the board hands control to the child, who learns to check it independently and manage the day.
