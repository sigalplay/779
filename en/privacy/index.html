Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Let's Play website collects, why it is collected and how you can control it.

Who is responsible for the information

The website is operated by Sigal Sasson-Spector. For privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete information, contact sigalsplay@gmail.com.

Information we collect

When you register, an email address and account identifier are stored. Your password is processed by Supabase authentication and is not shown to us in plain text. Your account may store favorites, folders and therapy plans. Other information entered into some tools may be stored only in your browser.

Therapist diary and client files

The therapist diary and client files are stored locally on the device and are not synced to the Supabase account. Use a first name or nickname only, without a surname, ID number, address, phone number or other identifying information. Clearing browser data may delete this information.

Children's photos

A photo uploaded to the story builder is processed locally on the device for cropping and background removal. By design, it is not sent to our server or stored in the website library, and is removed from the builder when you leave. Upload a photo only with a parent or guardian's permission.

Analytics and Cookies

Google Analytics is activated only after you select Accept in the Cookie notice. It helps us understand visits and pages viewed. You can decline analytics or change your choice through Cookie preferences in the website footer.

How we use information

Information is used to operate your account, save content you request, maintain security, provide support, fix errors and improve the website. We do not sell personal information or use your email for marketing without separate consent.

Service providers and data transfers

We use Supabase for accounts and cloud storage, and Google Analytics for analytics after consent. These providers may process information outside Israel under their terms and safeguards.

Retention, security and your rights

Account information is retained while the account is active or as needed for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to law. We use reasonable safeguards, but no system is completely secure. You may request access, correction or deletion using the contact address above.

Children

Account registration is intended for parents, guardians and professionals. A child should use the website with a responsible adult. We do not ask children to provide personal information directly to us.
