Rainbow Paper Towel Flower

Ages 4–10 20 minutes Moderate

Rainbow Paper Towel Flower

About This Activity

Make a flat paper-towel flower, add water to the center, and watch the colors spread toward the tips of the petals.

Materials
1Paper towel
2Pencil
3Scissors
4Water-based markers
5Plate
6Small Cup of Water
7Dropper

Preparation: Set out a dry plate, a small cup of water, and a dropper.

How to Play

1Draw a large flower with wide petals on a paper towel.

2Cut around the outline of the flower.

3Use the markers to color a thick ring around the center of the flower.

4Lay the flower flat on a dry plate.

5Use the dropper to add a few drops of water to the center. Watch the colors spread toward the tips of the petals.

Make it easier: Provide a pre-cut flower for the child to color and add water to.

Add a challenge: Make flowers in different sizes and predict which flower’s colors will reach the petal tips first.
