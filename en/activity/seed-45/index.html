I Packed My Suitcase

Ages 5–10 15 minutes Moderate

I Packed My Suitcase

About This Activity

A no-preparation memory game for car rides, bath time, and other waiting moments.

How to play: The first player says, ‘I went on a trip and packed…’ and names one item. The next player repeats that item and adds another. Keep extending the list until someone forgets the sequence.

Make it easier: For younger children, use a sequence of only two or three items and do not focus on mistakes.

Add a challenge: Add a rule that each new item must begin with the final sound or letter of the previous item.
