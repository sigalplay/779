Magic Salt Painting

Ages 5–10 25 minutes Moderate

Magic Salt Painting

About This Activity

Draw with glue, cover the lines with salt, then touch them with watercolor and watch the color travel.

Materials
1Thick paper or cardstock
2Bottle of white liquid glue with a nozzle
3Table salt
4Watercolors and brush
5Cup of water

Preparation: Cover the table with newspaper or plastic to collect excess salt.

How to Play

1Draw a simple picture in pencil.

2Trace the lines with an even stream of glue.

3While the glue is wet, cover every line generously with salt.

4Tilt the page over a bin or bowl to remove loose salt.

5Touch a very wet watercolor brush gently to one point and watch the color travel along the salt.

6Add different colors at several points until all lines are colored.

Tips:
  • ⭐ Draw letters, numbers, or the child’s name with glue to include learning practice.

Make it easier: Prepare one thick, simple glue line so the child can focus on adding salt and color.

Add a challenge: Use two or three colors and watch them meet and blend along the salt lines.
