Give the Character a Haircut

Ages 4–8 20 minutes Moderate

Give the Character a Haircut

About This Activity

Glue colorful yarn onto a character to create hair, then give the character a haircut.

Materials
1Printer
2Yarn in different colors
3Glue
4Scissors

Preparation: Print the prince or princess template, or draw a simple face on paper.

How to Play

1Cut pieces of yarn in different colors.

2Glue the yarn onto the page and allow it to dry.

3Plan a hairstyle and trim the yarn to the desired length.

Make it easier: Prepare equal-length pieces of yarn so the child can focus on gluing and the final haircut.

Add a challenge: Create several hairstyles on different pages or add decorations such as a ribbon or crown.
